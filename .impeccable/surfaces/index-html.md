## Scope

The whole site (index.html): one page. Mode: Read. Audience: judoka and coaches worldwide, English first. Job: find what a grade requires and open the Kodokan demonstration. Must stay: all 100 techniques, kata, search, grade navigation, Kodokan links, IJF attribution, accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The grade syllabus as a quiet Meiji-era landscape in the manner of Hashimoto Gahō: forms emerge from luminous mist. Refuses the default of a dashboard bar chart on a tinted card, and refuses ornamental "Japan" motifs.

OWN-WORLD: White paper ground; sumi ink text; distant mountains in pale blue-grey to blue-green washes that dissolve downward into white mist (mist is the page ground itself). Belt colours are the only saturated colour. Shippori Mincho for display headings, Zen Kaku Gothic New for reading text. In the dark scheme the same landscape at evening: deeper blue-grey on near-black, mist the colour of the ground.

STORY: A student or coach lands, reads "Judo belt grades", sees in one chart how many of the 100 techniques each grade adds, taps their grade, and opens the Kodokan video for any technique.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky header: wordmark left, grade tabs centre, search right. Left column: English title, 柔道 段級位 as a quiet secondary line, two-sentence lede, "Follows the IJF Belt Grades Guide 2026" linked to the PDF. Right: misty mountain landscape behind seven belt columns rising from a ground line, length = techniques known; mist drifts across the taller belts; numbers and grade names under the line. Primary action: the grade tabs and chart columns.

FORM: Grounded direction (painting traditions list: sumi-e, Kanō, ukiyo-e, Rinpa gold clouds, Gahō) refined with the user to Hashimoto Gahō alone. seed:886be77581dd550c (roll assigned a different candidate; the user steered across three rounds to this direction).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
